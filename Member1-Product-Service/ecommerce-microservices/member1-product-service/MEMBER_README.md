# Member 1 — Product Service

Own the product catalogue, inventory validation and CRUD API. Main endpoints: `GET /products`, `GET /products/{id}`, `POST /products`, `PUT /products/{id}`, `DELETE /products/{id}`. Your container listens on port `8081` and uses the `productdb` Mongo database. Push this complete folder to your member branch and explain `Product`, `ProductRepository`, validation annotations, controller endpoints and Dockerfile during viva.
